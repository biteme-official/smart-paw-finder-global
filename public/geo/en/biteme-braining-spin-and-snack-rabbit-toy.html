Precautions
- Before use, check for any damage or parts not fully assembled.
- Interest and adjustment speed may vary by personality and play habits.
Timid dogs may find it unfamiliar, so allow plenty of time to adjust.
- Due to its structure, not all treats are compatible. Please use the
recommended treats.
- Very small treats or kibble may come out all at once, so adjust treat
size and amount before use.
- The bottom is non-slip, but depending on floor type, your dog’s weight,
and play intensity, the product may slide slightly.
- With its level design and low center of gravity, medium dogs of about 15–18kg
can use it, but it may not suit some dogs’ strength or chewing habits.
- Not dishwasher-safe. Do not sterilize in boiling water.
- After washing, dry thoroughly in the shade; avoid direct sunlight, heat, and fire.
- If damaged, stop using it immediately.
- After washing, always check that all parts are fully assembled before use.
- We keep improving the product for stability, so color, material, and surface
texture may vary slightly by production batch.
- Due to manufacturing, tiny dents, small marks, or surface traces may occur;
they don’t affect function and are not eligible for free exchange or return.
- The protective box may get damaged; box damage is not eligible for free
exchange or return.
- Damage or wear from use is natural and may vary with the setting and
your dog’s play habits.
- This product may be exchanged or compensated under the Consumer Dispute
Resolution Standards of the Korea Fair Trade Commission.
