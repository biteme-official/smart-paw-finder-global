Precautions
- For pets only. Do not use for any other purpose.
- Take care on sensitive areas: belly, inner thighs, groin.
- Don’t force tangles; gently work them out from the ends.
- Pins can irritate skin, so keep them off the skin while
  brushing and gently sweep only the coat.
- Near wounds, eyes, mouth or anus, avoid direct contact
  and use with care.
- Don’t let your pet bite it or play with it like a toy.
- Pins are sharp; handle with care to avoid hand injuries.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- Be careful not to step on the product.
- Excessive force may partly damage the product.
- Avoid heat deformation and damage from impact.
- Do not clean or disinfect with hot water, alcohol, etc.
  This may damage the product.
- After use, remove hair and debris; store with pins dry.
- Keep the pins away from water.
- Store upright, pins off the floor; if laid flat,
  keep the pins facing up.
- Colors may vary slightly by production batch.
- Colors may vary by monitor resolution and photo conditions.
