Q&A
Q. Do I use it with a pee pad?
Yes! It's made to be placed on top of a pee pad.
Used with the non-slip silicone potty tray, it keeps pee
from running onto the floor.
Q. How is it different from a potty tray?
Instead of a tray's plastic top grid, it's made of
soft silicone. It prevents caught claws, with
no slipping and no noise.
Q. Does it fit every pad?
Based on Bosom Pad sizes, use 1 Zero Guard Mat
for a 50 X 40cm standard pad.
For a 75 X 60cm large pad, use 2 Zero Guard Mats.
Please check the size of the pad you use
before purchasing.
Q. How do I clean it?
Wash it with hot water once a day.
If it smells strongly, wash it with baking soda and a little
diluted bleach, then dry it in the sun in a well-ventilated spot.
The silicone can be sterilized in boiling water,
which keeps it even cleaner.
Q. My dog pees a lot. Will it still work?
For dogs who hold it and go all at once, or pee a lot,
we also recommend stacking 2 Zero Guard Mats.
Let your dog get used to the 6mm height first, then stack;
they will adapt faster and more naturally.
Q. I still see paw prints with the mat sometimes.
If there's more pee than usual,
your dog may step in it before the pad absorbs it.
As noted above, for dogs who pee a lot,
we recommend stacking Zero Guard Mats.
